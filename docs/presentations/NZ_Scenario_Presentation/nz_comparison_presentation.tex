\begin{frame}{Economic Interpretation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item NZ transition effects can be decomposed into demand reallocation, relative-price adjustment, and emissions-abatement dynamics.
  \item The 5-year grouped bars provide the main medium-term signal by averaging short-run volatility and highlighting persistent wedges versus baseline.
  \item GDP-level and investment-share deviations indicate how transition support and adjustment costs net out over successive policy windows.
  \item The split of final energy demand into grid and PV channels shows whether the transition relies more on centralized electrification or distributed energy uptake.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
