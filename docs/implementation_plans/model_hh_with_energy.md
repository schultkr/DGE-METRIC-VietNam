## Representative household (modified to make energy use explicit via durables + market energy)

### Preferences

A representative household derives utility from:
- consumption \(C_t\),
- housing services \(H_{t+1}\),
- **energy services \(S^E_t\)** (generated by an energy-using durable stock and market energy purchases),
and disutility from supplying labor \(N_{s,t}\) in period \(t\) across sectors \(s\in\{1,\dots,S\}\).

$$ U(C_t, H_{t+1}, S^E_t, N_{s,t}) = \frac{\left(C_t^{1-\gamma-\gamma_E}\, H_{t+1}^{\gamma}\, (S^E_t)^{\gamma_E}\right)^{\,1-\sigma^C}}{1-\sigma^C}
\;-\; \sum_{s=1}^{S} A^L_{s,t}\,\phi^L_s\,\frac{N_{s,t}^{\,1+\sigma^{L}}}{1+\sigma^{L}} $$

**Preference parameters and elasticities**
- \(\gamma\): preference weight of housing relative to consumption.
- \(\gamma_E\): preference weight of **energy services** relative to consumption and housing.
- \(\sigma^C\): governs intertemporal substitution in the composite good.
- \(\sigma^L\): inverse Frisch elasticity governing labor supply responses to real wages.
- Labor disutility scales with sector-specific labor productivity \(A^L_{s,t}\), reflecting human-capital requirements (Schultz, 1961; NBER w21381).
- Sector-specific disutility parameters \(\phi^L_s\) ensure realistic long-run labor shares across sectors.

---

### Energy services: durable + market energy

To make energy use explicit, I introduce:
- a **household energy durable stock** \(D_t\) (e.g., appliances/cooling/EV capital) accumulated via investment \(I^D_t\),
- **market energy purchases** \(E^H_t\) (e.g., electricity from the grid, fuels),
- an **energy-services aggregator** producing \(S^E_t\) from \((D_t, E^H_t)\).

A flexible CES specification is:
$$
S^E_t
=
\left[
\omega_D^{\frac{1}{\sigma_E}} D_t^{\frac{\sigma_E-1}{\sigma_E}}
+
(1-\omega_D)^{\frac{1}{\sigma_E}} (E^H_t)^{\frac{\sigma_E-1}{\sigma_E}}
\right]^{\frac{\sigma_E}{\sigma_E-1}}
$$
where \(\sigma_E\) governs substitution between “capacity” \(D_t\) and “utilization energy” \(E^H_t\).
(If I want “energy is required to operate the durable,” I can instead use a Leontief form.)

**Interpretation**
- \(D_t\) captures adoption of energy-using capital (efficiency, electrification, appliance stock).
- \(E^H_t\) is the **explicit** household demand for market energy.
- \(S^E_t\) is the service (comfort/mobility/lighting) entering utility.

---

### Choice variables

The household chooses:
- consumption \(C_t\),
- sectoral labor supply \(N_{s,t}\),
- sectoral investment \(I_{s,t}\) and next-period capital stocks \(K_{s,t+1}\),
- housing \(H_{t+1}\) (and housing investment \(I^H_t\)),
- **energy durable investment \(I^D_t\)** and **energy durable stock \(D_{t+1}\)**,
- **market energy purchases \(E^H_t\)**,
- and net foreign asset holdings \(B_{t+1}\).

---

### Problem

The household maximizes discounted lifetime utility:
$$
\max_{\{C_t,N_{s,t},I_{s,t},K_{s,t+1},I^H_t,H_{t+1},I^D_t,D_{t+1},E^H_t,B_{t+1}\}_{t\ge 0}}
\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\beta^t\,
U(C_t, H_{t+1}, S^E_t, N_{s,t})
$$

subject to the following constraints.

---

### Constraints

#### Budget constraint (extended with explicit energy and energy-durable investment)

$$
\text{Income}_t = \text{Expenditures}_t
$$

with the key modification that expenditures now include:
- energy-durable investment \(I^D_t\) at price \(P^{I,D}_t\),
- market energy purchases \(E^H_t\) at price \(P^E_t\).

A compact way to write this is:
$$
\text{Income}_t
=
P^C_t C_t
\;+\;
\sum_{s=1}^S P^{I}_{s,t} I_{s,t}
\;+\;
P^{I,H}_t I^H_t
\;+\;
P^{I,D}_t I^D_t
\;+\;
P^{E}_t E^H_t
\;+\;
\text{(net asset purchases and other expenditures)}_t
$$

(Everything else in the household budget—capital income, wage income, transfers/taxes, bond returns—remains as in the baseline model; I only make energy and the energy durable explicit.)

---

#### Sector-specific capital accumulation

$$
K_{s,t+1} = (1-\delta)\,K_{s,t} + I_{s,t}\,\Gamma_{s,t} - D^K_{s,t}
$$

---

#### Housing accumulation

$$
H_{t+1} = (1-\delta^H)\,H_t + I^H_t - D^H_t
$$

---

#### Energy-durable accumulation (new)

$$
D_{t+1} = (1-\delta^D)\,D_t + I^D_t - D^D_t
$$

where \(\delta^D\) is depreciation and \(D^D_t\) captures exogenous or disaster-related damages to the energy durable stock (optional, analogous to \(D^K_{s,t}\) and \(D^H_t\)).

---

#### Energy services definition (new)

$$
S^E_t = \mathcal{S}(D_t, E^H_t)
$$
with \(\mathcal{S}(\cdot)\) given by the CES aggregator above (or an alternative fixed-proportions form, depending on the interpretation I want).

---

### What this change buys me (model logic)

With this modification:
- household energy demand \(E^H_t\) becomes explicit and responds directly to the market energy price \(P^E_t\),
- adoption/efficiency/electrification policies can be modeled via \(P^{I,D}_t\), \(\delta^D\), or parameters of \(\mathcal{S}(\cdot)\),
- the model can distinguish between reducing energy use via **lower utilization** (lower \(E^H_t\)) vs. **higher efficiency/adoption** (higher \(D_t\) and/or improved \(\mathcal{S}(\cdot)\)).
